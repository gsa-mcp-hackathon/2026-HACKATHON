Pick a question below. The app pulls live records from FEMA's public database, applies one simple, published rule, and shows what it found and why.

The problem

After a disaster, FEMA's Public Assistance program pays to rebuild roads, schools and utilities. One disaster can produce thousands of project records. Reviewers cannot read them all, so unusual cases are easy to miss.

What this app checks

Three plain questions: Do road and bridge repairs cost far more than similar ones? Do utility repairs cost far more than similar ones? Is the same project listed more than once?

Why the evidence matters

A flag is a reason to look, not proof of wrongdoing. Every result shows how many records were checked, the exact rule used, and the original FEMA records, so a person can verify it.
